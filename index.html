Behind the work

Hi! I'm a Biomedical and Mechanical Engineering student at McMaster University, and I love the moment a system stops being just hardware and starts changing how someone moves through their day. Whether it's a wearable sensor tracking a child's gait or a linkage modeled to one patient's specific anatomy, I like starting from the clinical problem and designing backward into the mechanism.

I'm especially passionate about medical device development and rehabilitation technology, anywhere engineering can hand someone back a little more independence, and I'm always excited to find the next place biomedical engineering can actually help.
